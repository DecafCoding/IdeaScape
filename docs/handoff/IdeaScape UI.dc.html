19IdeaScape — the project picker, and the fetched card kinds

Two screens the plan needs and the old set never had. 19a is what opens first: a project is one folder on disk, so the picker is a recent list of folders and two ways to name a new one — no rail, no canvas, because nothing is open yet. 19b adds the two fetched kinds. A link card carries its preview picture, its site icon and its title; a video card carries a thumbnail, a duration and the provider, and clicking it opens the video in the browser rather than playing in place. Both fetch after the card is already on the canvas, so the panel shows the address it was made from and when the preview last came back. The second link card is the ordinary no-preview-data state — a title and an address, stated plainly and not as an error.

14IdeaScape — the canvas screen

The chosen shell for every screen: the project’s canvases and its search box stacked above the labelled tool rail on the left, the canvas on a dotted ground in the middle, and a properties panel on the right that fills in for whatever is selected — position, size, text and stacking are typed there rather than dragged. Cards sit wherever they were dropped; nothing snaps to a column. Connections carry the meaning: a line runs card edge to card edge, its arrow gives it direction, and a label rides the line where one is worth writing. Image cards are placeholders here and show their assets/ path.

17IdeaScape — right-click menus

Two menus, one grammar: rows are icon, label, shortcut, and the same actions carry the same shortcuts they do on the keyboard. Right-clicking a card selects it first, so the properties panel fills in behind the menu — 17a, with the destructive row last and set apart. 17b is the background menu; it creates at the pointer, not at the centre, so the coordinates under the cursor are where the new card lands, and Paste is greyed when the clipboard holds nothing IdeaScape can place. With nothing selected the properties panel collapses to a 32px edge — the caret reopens it — so the board gets the width back. Both menus duplicate what the rail and panel already offer — neither is the only way to reach an action.

16IdeaScape — Settings

The rail's gear replaces the canvas with a full-screen page; Back to canvas in the header returns to the board, and the gear stays lit while you are here. Changes apply as they are made — there is no Save or Cancel. Only the settings the plan actually has are here: the project folder, the save cadence, the grid, how zoom is driven, and the theme. Snapshots and a restore picker were cut — version history is deferred in the plan, and the undo stack lives in memory for the session, so there is nothing to configure. No account, no sync, no network — the file and privacy line sits at the foot of the page.

18IdeaScape — dark theme

The same two screens under Settings · Appearance · Dark. Nothing moves: every measurement, group and control is identical to 14b and 16a — only the ground inverts. Paper white becomes #1a1817, chrome and elements sit one step up at #262322, and ink is #ece9e6. Shadows do less work on a dark ground, so card separation leans on the surface step rather than the drop shadow, and the canvas dots and connection lines flip to a faint white. Cyan moves up its ramp to the 400 step so it still reads as the active colour against dark, and the delete magenta does the same.
